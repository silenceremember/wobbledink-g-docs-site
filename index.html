Wobbledink G-Docs

Terms and values from Google Sheets, displayed locally in Google Docs and Sheets.

Paste the link to an existing Google Sheets glossary and press Connect. The first two sheets contain term keys and definitions, and value keys and values, in columns A:B.

Use [term] and {value} markers. The extension displays matching labels and hover cards, including references inside definitions. Local display does not rewrite the stored Google Doc or replace markers in other spreadsheets.

The extension can add and remove glossary entries and repair the selected spreadsheet's layout. Repair formats the first two sheets and removes columns outside A:B. These actions change only the selected glossary spreadsheet.

The Google Sheets spreadsheets permission allows access to spreadsheets available to your Google account. The extension uses it to read and maintain the glossary at the link you provide. It does not request permission to edit Google Docs.
